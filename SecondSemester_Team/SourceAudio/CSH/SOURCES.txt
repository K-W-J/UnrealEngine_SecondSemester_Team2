CSH game SFX sources

AK47, Shotgun and Rotary use processed samples exported from the project's
existing /Game/Art/CSH/NW_MuzzleFX/Sound/Sfx/SW_5_56_new_01_v2 asset.
RPG and Strela use a short layer of the same sample with synthesized launch noise.
These are game sound designs, NOT verified recordings of those exact real weapons.
The original asset pack's license continues to apply to these sample-derived sounds.
Reference/ contains exports of that existing pack, not newly recorded audio.

Other sounds are generated from deterministic oscillators and filtered noise by
Tools/create_csh_audio.py. WaterGun is unpitched looping spray noise;
SciFiPistol, Sniper and FeignDeath use saturated low-mid energy and noise.
Explosion is a synthetic blast, low-frequency rumble and debris tail.

Workflow:
1. Unreal Python: Tools/export_csh_audio_sources.py (only needed to export originals).
2. Python 3: Tools/create_csh_audio.py
3. Unreal Python: Tools/import_csh_audio.py
4. Unreal Python: Tools/verify_csh_audio.py (read-only validation).
For commandlet import/export, include -AllowCommandletAudio to load audio decoders.
